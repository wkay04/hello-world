GROCERY LIST
============

PROTEINS (low effort)
- Rotisserie chicken (already cooked)
- Eggs
- Canned tuna or salmon
- Deli turkey/ham slices
- Greek yogurt

CARBS/GRAINS
- Bread
- Instant rice or microwavable rice cups
- Pasta
- Oatmeal (instant packets are fine)

FRUITS & VEGETABLES (low maintenance)
- Bananas
- Apples
- Baby carrots
- Bagged salad mix
- Frozen vegetables (steam-in-bag are easiest)

PANTRY BASICS
- Peanut butter
- Pasta sauce
- Olive oil
- Salt

SNACKS
- Nuts (almonds, mixed nuts)
- Cheese sticks or sliced cheese
- Crackers

DRINKS
- Water
- Coffee/tea
